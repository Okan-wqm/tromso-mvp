{% load i18n stock cards health %}{% comment %} A FITTED card (its template has a measure: monitoring/cards/resolve.py Resolved.measure): the same frame as any card, on two lines whatever happens, and the template's widgets in equal rows that share the card's height. Every length is one of four steps of the card's own unit (tw:text-u-s, -m, -l, -xl; static/css/monitor.css `.monitor-card.is-fit`), two weights. Line one: the unit and its link, its name (the department, and which template draws the card and why, in its title), the unit's status when it is not in use, the withdrawal with the first day of harvest (its batches and days are in its title; it is the one thing of the line that gives way when the line is full, from its end, so the code, the state and the day are never run over: the name gives way first), the flip link, the state's shape and word (what the state is from, in its title). Line two: a source that could not be read, then the FIRST reason of the state in short (monitoring/read.py Brief: the value against the bound it is past) and how many more there are; every reason is there in full for a screen reader and in the titles, and on the card the flip link shows. A card with no reason says its fish there; at its end, the link to the tank's water history (its icon: the full-height card has it in its head). A card of an empty unit says the unit itself in four cells (monitoring/_empty_cells.html): what is shown when there are no fish to show; a full-height card says it so in the new look only (the design's Big card of an empty unit: the one place a fitted card's markup differs between the looks; the classic Big card is as it was). GOING INTO A TANK (owner decision 7): on a card that is the overview's (fitted, not a full-height one; `row.open`, monitoring/views.py _openers) the unit's code is the link that opens its Big card in the pane beside the cards (`?focus=`; frontend/windows.ts docks it without a page load), and closes it again on the card that is open, which is outlined. A full-height card (the Big one, in the pane or on a wall of them) keeps the code's link to the unit's page and says IN SIGHT what the small card only has room to say in a title (review R11: a wall has no pointer): the department beside the name, and at its foot what the state is from and which template draws it.

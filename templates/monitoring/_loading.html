No state, no colour, no figure: a page whose cards have not come never reads as all is well. static/css/monitor.css shows one of its lines by the polled element's state: "Loading" while it is loading, "cannot be read" when the first answer failed ("unread": a 503, a server error, no answer), none when the person is signed out or may no longer read it ("gone": the page's own notice says so). Without script the grid's says where the full page is. {% endcomment %}

# ProductionUnits

Production › Operations › Production units: every tank, pond and cage with its batch, fish, biomass and density.

- Tabs with counts (Production 19, Empty 4, All 23); segmented filters for site and kind with pressed state in `action-tint` plus a 2px `action` ring.
- Table: Unit (sticky, code, place, a hold or critical chip under it), Batch, Fish, Avg. weight (g), Biomass (kg), Density and capacity as a `ui-gauge` (neutral fill until a limit, then `warn` / `crit`), bFCR, Survival (%), a sticky More button.
- Units in the column heads, figures right-aligned and tabular, one precision per column.
- Empty units fold into one row ("Empty units (4)…").

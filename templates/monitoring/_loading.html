{% load i18n %}{% comment %} What stands where the cards go before they have come (the shell, monitoring.views.monitor): the grid's (`grid`) or the pane's. Keyed, so the first answer of the poll (frontend/poll.ts, data-poll-state="loading" on the polled element) replaces it. No state, no colour, no figure: a page whose cards have not come never reads as all is well. static/css/monitor.css shows one of its lines by the polled element's state: "Loading" while it is loading, "cannot be read" when the first answer failed ("unread": a 503, a server error, no answer), none when the person is signed out or may no longer read it ("gone": the page's own notice says so). Without script the grid's says where the full page is. The grid's has a WATCHDOG line too, hidden, that the sheet shows after half a minute (CSS only, an animation's delay: it works when frontend/poll.ts never loads or never runs): a placeholder still there by then is one no answer has replaced, and the line is the way to the page drawn with its cards; the first answer takes it away with the placeholder. {% endcomment %}

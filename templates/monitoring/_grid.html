No chip is ever cut (review R4: an ellipsis ate the bound and "no recent data"): the strip wraps, on a wall too, where frontend/wall.ts gives the cards the room the band leaves. Every link and button of the band has an id: a poll replaces the whole band, and poll.ts gives the focus back to the element of the same id (review R8).
